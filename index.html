How Reverse Voice Party Game handles information and permissions during play.

1. Who is responsible

DMS Game Studios is responsible for personal data processed in connection with the app.

This policy applies to Reverse Voice Party Game and explains what information may be processed when you use it.

2. Data we process

Reverse Voice Party Game does not require an account. Player names and scores are kept for the current game session and are not sent to a game server. Your language, theme, sound and vibration settings, and content unlocked through rewarded ads are stored locally on your device.

3. Microphone and audio

When you choose to record and grant microphone permission, the app records the phrase for the game. You can manage or revoke microphone permission in your device settings; recording features will not work without it.

The recording is processed on the device to create reversed audio. The original and reversed clips are temporary files in the app's private storage. The game does not send audio clips to DMS Game Studios or to a game server.

4. How we use data

Recordings are used only for the voice gameplay features. The game does not use them for advertising or profiling. Google Mobile Ads is used separately to request and display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.

5. Sharing, ads and retention

The original recording is deleted after the reversed clip is created. Reversed clips are deleted at the end of a round or playground session, or when you leave that screen. If the app is stopped unexpectedly, a temporary file may remain in the device's temporary app storage until the app is opened again, when it is deleted. Saved settings and unlocks remain on your device until changed or the app is removed.

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads to serve ads. Depending on the device, region, consent choices and configuration, the SDK may collect and share IP address, device or advertising identifiers, diagnostic information and ad interactions for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. See Google's Android and iOS SDK data disclosures and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents consent or privacy choices. When the app makes the option available, you can revisit them in Settings → Manage ad privacy choices.

6. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict, port, or object to the processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.
